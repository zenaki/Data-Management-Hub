Start a diagram Import a .zip / .yaml project, load the sample, or add assets from the palette on the left. Drag the teal dot to connect assets, the purple dot to connect columns, and the corner of a container to resize it.
Properties

Select an asset, a column, or a line to edit it.

  • Drag an asset's header to move it; drop it inside a container to nest it.
  • Drag a container's header to move it with everything inside; drag its corner to resize.
  • Hover a block to see its 8 teal dots (left, right, 3 on top, 3 at the bottom). Drag one onto another asset to add a lineage relation; the arrow lands on the nearest dot there.
  • Drag a column's purple dot onto a column of another asset to make it a source.
  • Shift/Ctrl/⌘ + click to select several assets, or Shift + drag on empty space to box-select. Drag one of them to move them all.
  • Click a line, then drag its yellow end handles onto another block (or one of its dots) to change the upstream or downstream end.
  • Right-click a block, a column, or a line for Edit / Duplicate / Delete.
  • Keyboard (click the canvas first): Ctrl/⌘+D duplicate · Delete remove · Ctrl/⌘+A select all · Ctrl/⌘+Z undo · Esc clear.
  • Ctrl/⌘ + scroll to zoom; drag the background to pan.
